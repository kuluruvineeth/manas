# Manas

A 64M-parameter language model trained from scratch — its own tokenizer, its own pretraining
corpus, its own alignment. Everything you type here runs through weights we made ourselves.

It is small on purpose. Expect it to hold a conversation and get simple facts right, and expect
it to be confidently wrong about anything requiring real world knowledge. That gap is the lesson.

Use the gear icon to change sampling, cap the response length, or ask it to think before answering.
